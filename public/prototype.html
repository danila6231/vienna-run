Designer delivers Flat PNG illustrations: facades, landmark cutouts, items, waiter run frames 3D models (.glb) made in Blender, with each landmark modeled by hand Pixel-art sprites and one painted sky backdrop
How Viennese it reads Strong. Facade and landmark drawings can be as specific as the designer likes. Medium. Landmarks become toy versions and streets look generic. Medium. The skyline and sprites carry it at low detail.
Risk for the booth Low. New art is a PNG swap and the GPU load is light. Medium. Modeling takes the most time and shadows are the heaviest load on a weak PC. Lowest technical risk, but the hardest to make feel premium.

All three share the same rules: three lanes, collect treats, dodge Krampus and bombs, finish under the Riesenrad. There's no quiz or gift screen in these prototypes. Everything you see is placeholder art: items are emoji or simple shapes, and buildings and landmarks are drawn in code. Your designer's art would replace it.
